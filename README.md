ABOUT THIS PROJECT:

Age verification on social media is becoming a legal requirement across the EU, but the prodecure of uploading your ID for age verification creates serious privacy risks. This study compares Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs) against traditional ID upload methods to evaluate whether age can be verified without exposing personal data. We analyze both approaches through the lenses of cryptographic security, GDPR compliance, and technical feasibility.
